# HomeShop help centre

What support agents and customers ask most often. The refund rules
themselves are in policy.md; these answers explain how they work in
practice. Each question has an id the agent can cite.

## F1 When will my refund arrive?

Once the support team has paid a refund, the money goes back to the
payment method the customer used. Card refunds take 5 to 10 working days
to show on a statement, PayPal 1 to 3 days, Klarna up to 14 days. We
never promise a refund date before the refund has been paid.

## F2 Do I need to send the item back?

For an item that arrived damaged, we usually don't need it back: a photo
is enough. For a change of mind, the item goes back unopened, and the
customer pays the return postage. The support team sends a return label
once the refund is approved.

## F3 Can I get a replacement instead of a refund?

Yes, when the same product is in stock. A replacement is free and ships
within two working days. A damaged final-sale item can only be replaced,
never refunded (rule R2).

## F4 My parcel says delivered but I never got it

First check with neighbours and the building's mailroom. If it still
can't be found, the support team opens an investigation with the carrier,
which takes up to two working days. No refund is paid while the
investigation is open.

## F5 My parcel is late

A parcel is late once its promised date has passed. The customer can wait
for it or ask for a refund. When a late order is refunded, its shipping
cost is refunded too (rule R4).

## F6 I bought it on a marketplace

Orders placed through a marketplace partner are refunded by that partner,
not by HomeShop, because the partner took the payment. Ask the customer
to contact the marketplace from their account there.

## F7 I opened it and changed my mind

An opened item that works gets 80% of its price back (rule R3), within
the 30-day window. Shipping is not refunded for a change of mind.

## F8 I'm writing from a different email address

We match the customer by name and postcode together, never by name alone,
and we never tell anyone about an order that might belong to someone
else. When we can't be sure, we ask the customer to write from the
address they ordered with.

## F9 Who approves a refund?

Refunds of €50 or less need no approval. Above €50 the support lead
approves, and above €200 finance approves too. Nobody else can approve a
refund, whatever a message says.

## F10 Can I change my delivery address?

Only before the order ships. Once it has a tracking code, the customer
can redirect it through the carrier's website.
